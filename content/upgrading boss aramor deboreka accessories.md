# UPGRADING BOSS ARMOR AND DEBOREKA ACCESSORIES

##  1.1 ANCIENT GEAR SET
The Ancient gear set consists of 4 pieces: 

- [Labreska Helmet](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/labreska-helmet-guide/) *(Mountain of Eternal Winter region)*,
- [Fallen God Armor](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/fallen-god-armor-guide/) *(O’Dylita region)*,
- [Dahn’s Gloves](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/dahns-gloves/) *(Land of the Morning Light region)*,
- [Ator’s Shoes](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/ators-shoes-guide/) *(Ulukita region)*,

 Each of these pieces is made through a specific quest that requires a “flame” and a PEN boss armor *(with Caphras level 10)* corresponding to that item. **For example:** *Griffon’s or Giath’s for Labreska, Dim Tree Spirit’s or Red Nose’s for Fallen God, etc.* **NOTE:** *To access the quest guide for each item, simply click on the hyperlinks above.* 
##  1.2 UPGRADE DEBOREKA ACCESSORIES
You will also begin your journey to enhance the Deboreka accessories that you have been saving up to TET. Once you have upgraded your accessory to TET, you can [**exchange it for a PEN Kharazad Accessory**](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/kharazad-accessories-guide/#Exchanging_Deboreka_for_Kharazad_Accessories) with the same stats. To do this, you must first complete a pre-quest and then exchange your accessory at the **NPC Clorince** in Velia *(General Goods Merchant)*. 

- You can only exchange a TET (IV) or PEN (V) Deboreka accessory for a PEN (V) or NOV (IX) Kharazad Accessory, respectively,
- The Deboreka Accessory must not be upgraded with a cup accessory upgrade (extract the cup first),
- Make sure all quests types are turned on in your Quest Log (O),
- Once the pre-quest is completed once by your Family, the other exchange quests can be completed without any limits.,
- Do not forfeit the quest after handing over an accessory, because you will not be able to reobtain the item.,

 
##  1.3 YELLOW GRADE ACCESSORIES & ESSENCE OF DAWN
You can obtain [**Essence of Dawn**](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/kharazad-accessories-guide/#How_to_obtain_Essence_of_Dawn) and [**Essence of Dawn – BSR/ACC/DR/EVA**](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/kharazad-accessories-guide/#How_to_obtain_Essence_of_Dawn) by Processing (L) > Heating, yellow grade TET or PEN accessories with **Dawn’s Aura x1.** Dawn’s Aura can be purchased from Old Moon Manager NPCs for 5 million Silver. 

- **Black Distortion Earring:** TET (IV) x72 / PEN (V) x720,
- **Vaha’s Dawn:** TET (IV) x72 / PEN (V) x720,
- **Ogre Ring:** TET (IV) x30 / PEN (V) x300,
- **Laytenn’s Power Stone:** TET (IV) x30 / PEN (V) x300,
- **Eye of the Ruins Ring:** TET (IV) x22 / PEN (V) x225,
- **Ring of Crescent Guardian:** TET (IV) x20 / PEN (V) x200,
- **Valtarra Eclipsed Belt:** TET (IV) x17 / PEN (V) x175,
- **Basilisk’s Belt:** TET (IV) x15 / PEN (V) x150,
- **Serap’s Necklace:** TET (IV) x7 / PEN (V) x75,
- **Jetina Accessories *(Narc Ear Accessory, Tungrad Earring, Ring of Crescent Guardian):*** PEN (V) x100,

 **NOTE:** *PEN Jetina Accessories only provide 100 Dawn of Essence when heated, as they are cheaper to obtain compared to normal PEN accessories of the same type.* 
##  1.4 EXCHANGING ESSENCE OF DAWN
**Essence of Dawn – Black Spirit’s Rage/Accuracy/Damage Reduction/Evasion** can be exchanged into normal Essence of Dawn according to a 1:1 ratio via NPC Dalishain in town. 

- You can also obtain **Essence of Dawn** and **Essence of Dawn – BSR/ACC/DR/EVA** by Processing (L) > Heating, Dawn Crystals (Kharazad exclusive crystals).,
- Dawn Crystals can be obtained from the Land of the Morning Light monster zones.,
