# Hunting

![](https://i.ibb.co/QFzWYyHk/image.png)

> Beginner to Advanced Hunting Guide

This compilation guide outlines the complete progression path for the **Hunting Life Skill**, covering initial workshop setup, matchlock crafting, early-to-mid level progression spots, fast travel strategies, essential consumable buffs, and advanced **Land of the Morning Light (Sangcheul / Bamboo Valley)** hunting mechanics.

---

## 1. Initial Setup: Workshop & Worker Node Allocation

To begin producing matchlocks independently without relying solely on Central Market listings, establish a designated **Tool Workshop** and hire a dedicated worker.

### Setup Instructions
1. **Choose a Major Town:** Cities such as **Glish** (or Heidel/Velia) provide accessible Tool Workshops.
2. **Rent a Tool Workshop:** Open the City Map node, invest Contribution Points (CP) in a residential property, and convert/upgrade it to a **Tool Workshop (Level 1+)**. To craft the best matchlocks a Level 4 Tool Workshop is required, Glish is a popular choice for this.
3. **Hire a Worker:**
   * Speak to the **Work Supervisor NPC** (e.g., *Bach* in Glish).
   * Hire a worker (Goblins are preferred for faster crafting speed). However crafting can always be force finished with silver/pearls.

## 2. Matchlock Progression & Required Materials

Matchlocks unlock progressively based on your character's **Hunting Life Skill Level**. Upgrade them sequentially to increase damage, accuracy, and reload efficiency.

### Material Requirements Matrix

| Matchlock Tier | Required Hunting Level | Key Crafting Ingot / Timber | Additional Ingot / Materials |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Beginner Matchlock** | Beginner Lv. 1+ | **Brass Ingot** | **Bronze Ingot**, Log / Softwood |
| **Apprentice Matchlock** | Apprentice Lv. 1+ | **Bronze Ingot** | **Steel**, Timber |
| **Skilled Matchlock** | Skilled Lv. 1+ | **Steel Ingot** | **Platinum Ingot** |
| **Professional Matchlock**| Professional Lv. 1+| **Platinum Ingot** | **Gold Ingot**, Trace Materials |
| **Artisan Matchlock** | Artisan Lv. 10+ | **Gold / Master Ingot** | **Supreme Materials** |

> **Pro-Tip (Market Shortcut):** While crafting via your Tool Workshop is cost-effective, high-tier matchlocks (such as **+10 Artisan Matchlock**) are frequently available on the **Central Market**. Purchasing one directly saves significant time.

### Matchlock Enhancement
* Enhance matchlocks using **Black Stones** via the Black Spirit interface (`/`).
* Maximum standard safe enhancement level is **+10**.
* **Durability Repair:** Use **Memory Fragments** or identical base matchlocks at any Blacksmith to restore maximum durability after failed enhancements or usage.

## 3. Fast Travel Strategy: The Magnus Network

Hunting grounds are distributed across distant regions. Minimizing travel time between your base and hunting spots is crucial for efficiency.

* **Abyssal Veins (Abyss One: The Magnus):** Complete *The Magnus* questline early to unlock direct teleportation wells between major cities (e.g., Heidel, Calpheon, O'dyllita).
* **Local Teleportation:** Jump into the nearest Magnus well to bypass long manual horse journeys when transitioning between your Tool Workshop, Central Market, and hunting fields.

## 4. Early & Mid-Game Progression Spots

### Primary Spot: O'dyllita / Rhino Grounds (Narcion Area)
* **Location:** South/East region near **Narcion** (O'dyllita).
* **Target Mobs (Beginner to Skilled):** **Verdure Buck, Verdure Doe** and **Shadow Wolves**.
* **Target Mobs (Artisan 10+ with High Matchlock):** **Shadow Wolves**, **Wild Gazelles** and **Rhinos**.

### Leveling Roadmap
1. **Phase 1 (Beginner to Skilled):** Equip a Beginner/Apprentice Matchlock and farm **Verdure Buck, Verdure Doe** near Narcion. They have low HP pools and allow rapid Hunting EXP accumulation.
2. **Phase 2 (Skilled to Artisan 10):** Upgrade to a **Skilled or Professional Matchlock**. Transition to hunting **Shadow Wolves** and **Rhinos** to increase overall EXP rate per kill.
3. **Phase 3 (Artisan 10+ End-Game Base):** Equip an **Artisan Matchlock (+10)**. You can now clear high-tier **Grass Rhinos** and **Boss-type Mobs** for massive yield profits, including **Caphras Stones**, **Sharp Black Crystal Shards**, **Custom Furniture**, and **Meat/Hide**.

## 5. Essential Buffs & Consumables

Hunting damage and attack speeds do **not** depend on standard class AP/AAP. Instead, matchlock reload speed, critical hit chance, and special attack extra damage are influenced by specific food/elixir buffs.

### Mandatory Food Buff
* **Exquisite Cron Meal:** Non-negotiable for Hunting.
  * Grants **Attack Speed +8%**, **Movement Speed +8%**, **Critical Hit Damage +8%** and **Back Attack Extra Damage +5%**.

### Recommended Elixirs & Extra Buffs
1. **Perfume of Swiftness / Elixir of Flowing Time:** Increases Movement Speed and Life EXP.
2. **Life Spirit Stone / Treant Tear:** Grants extra Life EXP and Gathering/Hunting speed bonuses.
3. **Destruction Spirit Stone:** Grants +% Attack Speed if extra Life EXP from Life Spirit/Alchemy Stone isn't needed.
4. **Hunting Clothes:** Equip **Loggia Hunting Clothes** or **Robeau Hunter's Clothes** or **Manos Hunting Clothes** to boost Hunting Mastery and overall item drop rate from butchering hunted game as well as to increase DP to withstand Hunting mobs attacks.

## 6. Advanced Spot: Land of the Morning Light (Sangcheul / Bamboo Valley)

Once you reach high-tier Hunting levels and possess an upgraded matchlock, you can transition to the premier end-game hunting region in **Sangcheul / Bamboo Valley**.

### Prerequisites & Instant Spot Entry
1. **Main Questline:** Fully complete the **Land of the Morning Light - Sangcheul / Tale of the Imoogi** main storyline quests.
2. **Boss Shrine Shortcut (Instant Teleport):**
   * Open the **Black Shrine Interface** (ESC Menu -> Black Shrine).
   * Select **Imoogi** and click **Enter / Start Match**.
   * As soon as the loading screen finishes and the cutscene/instance initializes, press **ESC** and **Cancel / Exit Match**.
   * **Result:** The system places your character directly into the **Bamboo Valley / Sangcheul Hunting Zone** without requiring manual travel or horse rides across the continent.

## 7. Matchlock Sniper & Active Aiming Mechanics

Hunting in Land of the Morning Light uses an active scope aiming mechanic that rewards precise timing over standard point-and-shoot methods.

### Aiming Step-by-Step
1. **Scope View:** Press **Right Click** with your matchlock equipped to enter scope mode.
2. **Charging the Bar:** Hold **Left Click**. An active timing bar will begin filling under your reticle.
3. **The Gold Zone (Perfect Shot):** Release **Left Click** exactly when the bar reaches the **Yellow / Gold indicator**.
   * **Result:** Triggers a **Perfect Shot / Critical Shot**, dealing massive single-target damage and usually one-shotting the target.

### Target Behaviors
* **Wild Boars:** Highly resilient (often take 2–3 shots depending on mastery/weapon). A perfect headshot can kill them instantly. Boars charge directly at you, but their attacks **do not interrupt** your matchlock aiming state.
* **Bears:** Slower movement, but their attacks and roars **can interrupt** your shot charging state if they hit you before you fire. However, they possess lower HP pools than Boars and die faster.

## 8. Mastery Scaling & Yield Breakdown

A common misconception among players is that high-end Land of the Morning Light hunting requires maximum end-game mastery gear.

* **Mastery Threshold Test:** Testing yields between **~1,300 Mastery** (basic gear / no accessories) and **~2,200 to 3,000 Mastery Cap** shows **negligible differences in core drop quantities**.
* **Core Drop Yields (Per Kill):**
  * **Caphras Stones:** ~3 to 6+
  * **Sharp / Hard / Concentrated Black Crystals:** 1 to 4+
  * **Sangcheul / Breath of Morning Materials:** High drop consistency across all mastery tiers.
* **Conclusion:** Beginner/Intermediate players with **~1,300 Mastery** can farm the spot at near-peak financial efficiency without investing billions in high-tier Manos/Preonne gear.

## 9. Profit Avenues & Mythical Horse (Tier 9/10) Progression

Hunting in Bamboo Valley produces high-value regional drops that fuel both direct silver gains and end-game horse awakenings.

### Key Drops & Profit Streams
1. **Breath of Morning (Sangcheul):** Essential crafting component used to produce high-tier Life Skill gear, accessories, and specialized elixirs.
2. **Mythical Horse Awakening Materials (Tier 9 / Dream Horses):**
   * Drops from this zone provide the required materials for **Mythical Arduanatt / Mythical Horse Awakening attempts**.
   * **Silver Potential:** Crafting and selling full attempt materials on the market or selling completed horses yields up to **50 Billion Silver (50B+)** per completed Tier 9/10 progression milestone.
3. **Furniture & Stuffed Heads:** Drops rare **Stuffed Wild Boar Heads** and **Stuffed Bear Heads**, which grant potent AP/Mastery buffs when placed in player housing or sold on the Central Market.
