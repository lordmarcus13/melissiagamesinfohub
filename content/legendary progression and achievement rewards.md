# LEGENDARY PROGRESSION AND ACHIEVEMENT REWARDS

![](https://i.imgur.com/rIJVJMd.png)

Melissia Games now offers players exclusive, prestigious, high-tier progression and achievement rewards. There are three legendary achievements available:

1. Reaching Level 70 on all available classes ➡ Rewards **25x** [GM Medals](https://bdocodex.com/us/item/16655/).
2. Achieving "Maxed-Gear" status on all available classes ➡ Rewards **15x** [GM Medals](https://bdocodex.com/us/item/16655/).
3. Reaching the maximum level (Guru 100) in all Life Skills except Trading ➡ Rewards **15x** [GM Medals](https://bdocodex.com/us/item/16655/).

> Every 5 GM Medals allows you to claim any single in-game item, piece of equipment, or Webshop content of your choice including Voltarion horse and Boosted Alpaca.

#### **Must Read: Learn more about GM Medals and the reward system by [clicking here](https://melissiagamesinfohub.vercel.app/en/wiki/gm-medal-rewards).**

---

## ALL 29 CLASSES AT LEVEL 70

If a player successfully levels all 29 classes to Level 70, they will be rewarded with **25x** [GM Medals](https://bdocodex.com/us/item/16655/). 

> Players who complete this achievement must submit a claim to the staff.
> @TyBoo will review the applicant's account to verify the achievement. Once confirmed, the reward will be distributed.
 
## ALL 29 CLASSES MAXED-GEAR

If a player achieves "Maxed-Gear" status on all 29 classes, they will be rewarded with **15x** [GM Medals](https://bdocodex.com/us/item/16655/).

### Definition of "Maxed-Gear"

A character must have all of the following items equipped to be considered "Maxed-Gear":
1. 6x Fully Reformed DEC Kharazad Accessories (2x Earring, 2x Ring, 1x Belt, 1x Necklace)
2. 4x OBLITERATING Slumbering Origin Armor Pieces (1x Fallen God's Armor, 1x Labreska's Helmet, 1x Ator's Shoes, 1x Dahn's Gloves)
3. 2x DEC Sovereign Weapons (1x Main Weapon, 1x Awakening Weapon)
4. 1x PEN Blackstar Sub-Weapon OR PEN Godr-Ayed Sub-Weapon OR PEN Boss Sub-Weapon (Caphras Level 20)

> Each of the 29 classes must individually possess all the items listed above. No pieces can be missing or shared. You must have 29 entirely separate, fully-geared characters.

> Players who complete this achievement must submit a claim to the staff.
> @TyBoo will review the applicant's account to verify the achievement. Once confirmed, the reward will be distributed.
 
## ALL LIFE SKILLS AT GURU 100

If a player successfully raises every Life Skill **EXCEPT TRADING** to the maximum level of Guru 100, they will be rewarded with **15x** [GM Medals](https://bdocodex.com/us/item/16655/). 

> Players who complete this achievement must submit a claim to the staff.
> @TyBoo will review the applicant's account to verify the achievement. Once confirmed, the reward will be distributed.

---

## TERMS AND CONDITIONS:

1. Players can complete and claim multiple achievements. (e.g., A player who reaches Level 70 on all classes, reaches Guru 100 in all Life Skills, and maxes out the gear on all 29 characters will earn 25 + 15 + 15 = **55x** [GM Medals](https://bdocodex.com/us/item/16655/)).
2. Each achievement reward can only be claimed **once per account**.
3. If a future game client update introduces new classes, you cannot claim the "All Classes" rewards a second time simply by leveling or gearing the newly added classes.
4. Players cannot exploit the system to claim the same rewards multiple times by changing their Family Name or other account details.
5. All accounts claiming these achievements will undergo a strict manual review by the staff.
6. There is no player limit, time limit, or ranking requirement. Any account can apply for these achievement and progression rewards at any time once the criteria are met.
