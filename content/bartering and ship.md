# Bartering and Ship

![](https://i.ibb.co/671Ptj1N/image.png)



Whether you are just stepping onto a boat for the first time or looking to optimize your existing trade routes, this guide will cover everything from selecting the right ship to maximizing your daily silver gains.

---

## 1. Choosing Your Ship: Why Not a Carrack?
For standard bartering and maximizing profit-per-hour, you might think the Tier 5 Carrack is the ultimate goal. However, **we do not recommend starting with or focusing heavily on a Carrack for pure bartering**. 

* **The Problem with Carracks:** Building a Carrack requires massive material investments, daily quests, and around a week of solid grinding just to get the base materials—not to mention the cost and time of crafting its +10 blue gear. 
* **The Starting Alternative:** Start with an **Epheria Sailboat** or **Epheria Caravel**. You can either build one or place a pre-order on the Central Market. These are perfectly adequate for beginning your bartering journey and building up your base materials (Tier 1 to Tier 3 goods). You can also exchange Shakatu Seals for an Epheria Sailboat.

---

## 2. The Ultimate Bartering Goal: Epheria's Star and The Special Ship
As you barter, you trigger "Special Barters" or "Sudden Barters." These semi-rare events allow you to trade standard goods like Gold Bars for ultra-rare items like **Garmoth's & Vell's Heart**, Khan's Energy, rare lightstones, Tri and Tet Manos accessories, Crow Coins (up to 10k!), Carrack gear boxes, Barter & Sailing Artifacts, a delivery quest rewarding 100 Oquilla's Flowers, or 1-hour PVP elixirs granting additional AP/DR. 

Among these drops is the ultimate prize for a barterer: **Epheria's Star**.

### Unlocking the Special Ship
1. Once you obtain **Epheria's Star** from a Special Barter, a questline will unlock.
2. Travel to **Epheria Port** and speak with the NPC **Philaberto Falasi**.
3. Upon completing the quest, you will be rewarded with a unique title ("Captain Epheria") and an **exclusive, massive ship**. the **Epheria Galleon**

### Why is this Special Ship Better?
This ship is the undisputed king of bartering:
* It requires **no gear** to be equipped.
* Its base speed is an astonishing **200% to 210%**, making it substantially faster than a fully-geared Carrack.
* It features unique visual animations (dolphins swimming alongside you, a "time-tunnel" visual effect when using the Breezy Sail skill).
* **Important Note:** To maximize this ship's speed, you must increase your Sailing Mastery. Equip **Manos Sailing Clothes** and a **Loggia/Srulk/Manos Sailing Log** (even at base enhancement, these provide significant speed boosts of 10-15%).
* **Pro Tip:** The Corsair classes passive skill *Sea Lungs* provides an extra +1% increase to all Big Ship stats. This includes enhancements to your ship's Speed, Acceleration, Rotation, and Brake.

## 3. How to Barter: The Basics
Bartering is essentially a chain of trades: you trade base materials (like raw timber or dough etc.) for Tier 1 sea goods, then trade Tier 1 for Tier 2, and so on, all the way up to Tier 5. 

### The Process
1. Open your Barter Information window to see what islands demand what materials. For example, an island might ask for *Corn Dough* in exchange for a *Tier 1 good*, and another might ask for *Pine Plywood*.
2. **Loading the Ship:** You can load your ship directly from your storage. Make sure your base materials are in the ship's inventory, not your character's inventory.
3. **Sailing and Trading:** Sail to the specific node/island. You do not need to disembark. Simply click the "Anchor" button when prompted near the island and select the "Barter" option.
4. **The Exchange:** Trade your materials. 
   * **Tier 1 (White) to Tier 2 (Green) to Tier 3 (Blue):** These trades do not yield silver directly; they build your inventory of trade goods.
   * **Tier 3 (Blue) to Tier 4 (Yellow):** This is where the profit begins. In the current economy, turning in Blue goods for Yellow goods nets you approximately **400 Million Silver per exchange**. A full run of 10 exchanges yields **4 Billion Silver**.
   * **Tier 4 (Yellow) to Tier 5 (Orange):** Exchanging Yellow goods for Orange goods yields **1 Billion Silver per exchange**, meaning a full run yields **6 Billion Silver**.

### Remote Collection Trick (Quality of Life)
Normally, if you remote-collect a ship at a Wharf Manager, the trade goods inside are destroyed. However, the current system allows you to remote-collect your ship *without losing the barter goods inside*. 
* This means you can use a Traveler's Map to teleport back to a main city (like Iliya Island), remote collect your ship, and all your heavy Tier 4/Tier 5 items will safely remain in the ship's hold, saving you massive amounts of travel time.

## 4. Scaling Up and Making Billions
The core gameplay loop of bartering is incredibly simple but highly rewarding. 

1. **Daily Limits:** You can refresh your Barter Board up to 3 times (yielding 4 total boards) a day, allowing you to run multiple routes. 
2. **AFK vs. Active:** You can set an auto-path (T) and step away from the keyboard, letting the ship sail itself. Alternatively, if you actively use the "Breezy Sail" skill (Shift + W), you will arrive much faster. (Note: Using skills requires your Sailing life skill to be at least Skilled 1).
3. **Profits:** Because each high-tier trade yields 400M to 1B silver, maximizing your daily barter refreshes can easily net you tens of billions of silver purely from AFK/Semi-AFK sailing up to 250 billions per day.

### Summary Checklist for New Barterers:
* Buy an Epheria Sailboat/Caravel off the Central Market to start.
* Build a stockpile of Tier 1 to Tier 3 goods.
* Always check Special Barters for the **Epheria's Star**.
* Once you have the Special Ship, equip Sailing Mastery gear for raw speed.
* Use the Remote Collection trick to save travel time.
* Trade up to Tier 4 and Tier 5 goods for billions of silver.

Happy sailing, Captain!
