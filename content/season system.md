# Melissia Games Season System Guide

![](https://i.ibb.co/23gncsHR/image.png)


## 🔸 **IS THERE A TIME LIMIT TO STAY IN SEASON?**

Yes, there is. Seasonal Channel is accessible for the 30 days for new players. For returning players (who have not logged in for more than 90 days), the time is 15 days.

## 🔸 HOW DOES THE SEASON SYSTEM WORK ON MELISSIA?

You can check [Naked to Graduated](https://melissiagamesinfohub.vercel.app/en/wiki/naked-to-graduated) to get the detailed season guide and finish the season in 2 hours. **Melissia** gives you the freedom to create multiple Season characters on your account by using the **Season Expansion Coupon**. Due to this, you can also claim season rewards multiple times. However, keep in mind that you can only have **one active Season character at a time**. If you wish to create a new one, you must first graduate your currently active Season character. 
 
## 🔸 HOW MUCH TIME DOES IT TAKE TO FINISH THE SEASON?

If you follow [Naked to Graduated](https://melissiagamesinfohub.vercel.app/en/wiki/naked-to-graduated) you can finish the season in 2 hours.

## 🔸 HOW TO GRADUATE THE SEASON?

Once you complete the Season Pass and claim all rewards, the Graduate option will automatically appear in the pass window. Click this to graduate your character. If clicking Graduate does not trigger the graduation, leave the Season Server, switch to a normal server, and click Graduate again
 
## 🔸 CAN I FORCE GRADUATE TO LEAVE THE SEASON EARLY?

Yes, you can do this at any time. There is an option at the bottom of the Season Pass window where you can do this. However, if you are a new player, we do **not** recommend doing so, as there is no real benefit in giving up your Season rewards just to graduate early. Remaining in the Season allows you to fully take advantage of the progression bonuses, materials, and exclusive rewards available during the Season period. Additionally, keep in mind that drop rates, EXP gains, and other bonuses are higher on the Season server. 
 
## 🔸 WHICH STARTING ZONE SHOULD I CHOOSE?

[Naked to Graduated](https://melissiagamesinfohub.vercel.app/en/wiki/naked-to-graduated)
If you are a new player and wants a faster and smoother experience, the only viable option is **Ancient Stone Chamber**. By starting there, you will have access to several beginner grind spots and can focus purely on gaining EXP by killing monsters, if you prefer. On the other hand, if you choose **Land of the Morning Light** or **Mountain of Eternal Winter**, keep in mind that your progression will be slower and heavily gated behind a long questline. 
 
## 🔸 DO I HAVE TO DO ALL THE MAIN QUEST LINE?

The game currently offers the "[Simplified Main Quest](https://www.blackdesertfoundry.com/simplified-questline-guide/)" system, which allows both Season and regular characters to skip the entire main questline up to Mediah. Due to this, you can progress purely by killing monsters and leveling up, without being forced to complete the main story quests.

## 🔸 MY SEASON SERVER ACCESS HAS EXPIRED. CAN I STILL CREATE SEASON CHARACTERS AND PLAY ON NORMAL SERVERS?

Yes, you can still create season characters and play on normal servers by buying season character ticket from F3 once and more from webshop.

## 🔸 HOW CAN I GET TIME-FILLED BLACK STONES FOR SEASON CHARACTER ON NORMAL SERVER?

There are quests where you can get Time-filled Black Stone in Season Pass.
For example;
- With one of them, you get 200 by upgrading Naru to TRI level.
- By converting PEN Naru to PRI Tuvala, you get 400 more.
- At level 52, 200.
- Without using Time-filled Black Stone you already got 800 (1600 with pass)
- By doing an enhancement with the stones you got (weapon), 500 more.
- By enhancing one armor DUO, 400 more.

With these amounts, you get a self-sustaining loop and you can fully max a season character very easily. not even mentioning that if you have Black Spirit Pass, it will double this amount
