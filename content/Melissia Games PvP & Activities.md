# ✦ Melissia Games PvP & Activities ✦


### ⚔️ RBF (Valencia, Cron Castle)
- **Silver Rewards:** Win 4B / Loss 1B
- **Weekend Rewards:** Win 8B / Loss 2B (Amount of items received increased to Gold Bar 1,000G: 40/80 for win, 10/20 for loss).

### 🛡️ Arena Of Solare System
- **Silver Rewards:** Win 2.5B (Gold Bar 1,000G x25). 
- **Weekend Bonus:** Winning awards +10 rating points.
- **Monthly Rank Rewards:** 
  - *801-1200 points:* Gold Bar 10,000G x15
  - *1201-1500 points:* Gold Bar 10,000G x25
  - *1501-1800 points:* Gold Bar 10,000G x50
  - *1801-2100 points:* Premium (15d), Essence of Gallantry x50, Essence of Honor x50
  - *2101-2400 points:* Premium (30d), Essence of Gallantry x75, Essence of Honor x75
  - *2401+ points:* Premium (45d), Essence of Gallantry x100, Essence of Honor x100
  *(Rewards sent via mail. Highest rank across characters is used.)*
