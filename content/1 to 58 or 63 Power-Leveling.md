# 1 to 58 or 63 Power-Leveling

For power-leveling from 1 to 58 or 63, creating a Season Character is heavily recommended for new players. The primary reasons for this are the significantly higher EXP rates and the progression-boosting gear provided by seasonal servers.

## Normal Character

The core process is functionally similar. To level up a standard character, follow this route:

1. **Level 1 to 18–30:** Cron Castle
2. **Level 18–30 to 58:** Helms Post
3. **Post-58 Routing Options:**
   * 3.1. Push to level 61 using Marni's Stones at Polly's Forest.
   * 3.2. Proceed to one of the two advanced alternative paths: [Bloody Monastery](https://melissiagamesinfohub.vercel.app/en/wiki/leveling-arsha-elvia-bloody-monastery-crimson-bell-loop) or [Savage Rift](https://melissiagamesinfohub.vercel.app/en/wiki/leveling-savage-rift-unique-easiest).
