# Leveling: Combat EXP Buffs

Reaching Lv. 70 offers minor statistical advantages but serves as a prestigious milestone for dedicated players. While Lv. 66 represents the "soft cap" and is easily attainable, Combat EXP gains drop significantly afterward, requiring a highly optimized strategy. By stacking the correct buffs, you can push from Lv. 66 to Lv. 70 in a matter of days. 

This guide covers the foundational Combat EXP buffs necessary to maximize your progression before you step into your preferred grinding method.
To optimize your leveling speed, you must stack as many Combat EXP buffs as possible. When combined, these buffs can yield a total of **+3748% Combat EXP** or up to **+4198%** if utilizing [Skeleton Pet](https://melissiagamesinfohub.vercel.app/en/wiki/pet-system).
