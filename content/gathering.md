# Gathering

![](https://i.ibb.co/1fmR7V7z/image.png)

This comprehensive guide details the most efficient approach for gathering.

---

## 1. Overview & Location

While traditional gathering methods—such as logging, mining, or hoe gathering—are effective, they incur high weight penalties, requiring frequent storage trips, maid usage, and heavy inventory management.

### Recommended Location: Lynch Ranch Sheep Mountain
* **Location:** Directly south of Lynch Ranch (near Heidel / Northern Plain of Serendia).
* **Target Node:** Mountain Sheep / Sheep Herds.
* **Key Advantage:** High density of tightly grouped mobs, minimal weight burden (primarily blood or meat), and zero long-distance movement between packs.

---

## 2. Recommended Class Setup & Skills

Classes with wide-area **vacuum / grouping skills** significantly accelerate efficiency by pulling dense herds of sheep into a single point before harvesting.

### Preferred Classes
* **Shai:** Highly recommended as a dedicated Life Skill alt (native passive weight capacity, gathering bonuses, and strong area control).
* **Musa / Maehwa:** Excellent mobility and fast grouping mechanics.

### Essential Skill Setup
* **Shai Skill:** *Gather 'Round!* (Level requirement: Lv. 40+).
* **Usage:** Map the skill to a hotbar key. Activating it instantly vacuums surrounding sheep into a single stacked pile, eliminating the need to move between individual carcasses.

---

## 3. Gathering Mechanics & Buff Management

### Recommended Tool
* **Fluid Collector:** Recommended for maximum Caphras throughput without heavy weight accumulation. Fluid Collecting also has the fastest gathering mini game.
* **Butcher Knife:** Alternative choice if meat is also required for Cooking.

### Essential Buffs & Items
1. **Item Increase Scroll (Level 2):** Grants **+100% Item Drop Amount** and **+50% Drop Rate**.
2. **Agris Fever:** Toggle ON to maximize item yields per gathering action.
3. **Lifestone / Life Spirit Stone & Seafood Cron Meal:** For gathering speed +5, gathering mastery boost, and energy recovery rate.
4. **Fresh Whale Meat Salad:** Adds an extra +15% Life EXP and can be stacked with Seafood Cron Meal if used after.
5. **Verdure Draught:** Every lifeskillers favorite draught, +20% Life EXP as well as other benefits for other lifeskills.
6. **Perfume of Swiftness:** +20% Life EXP, +200LT Weight and +5 Movement Speed
7. **Secret Book of Florin:** +100 Life Skill Mastery, +20% Life EXP
8. **Lara's Warm Black Tea:** +30% Life EXP, Gathering Item Drop Rate +10%, +100LT Weight

### Mini-Game Optimization
* **Gathering Mini-Game (Green Thumb):** When triggered, completing the quick-time event grants **10x base yields** in a single interaction. This bonus stacks with Agris Fever and the passive effect of Golden Hedgehog and Webshop Hedgehog for even higher yields.
* **Yield Breakdown (Per Mini-Game Trigger):**
  * **Caphras Stones:** ~3 to 6 per trigger.
  * **Hard / Sharp Black Crystal Shards:** 1 to 3+ per trigger.
  * **Base Materials (Blood/Meat):** 30 to 50+ per trigger.

## 4. Mastery Targets & Yield Expectations

Caphras Gathering scales directly with **Gathering Mastery**.

| Gathering Mastery | Caphras Yield / Hr (Avg) | Effort Level | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **~1,200 Mastery** | High | Low / Budget Gear | Easily accessible with Tet/Pen Loggia or Tri Manos setup. |
| **~1,700+ Mastery** | Very High | End-Game | Maximize yield via Caphras Drop Rate scaling. |

> **Note:** Caphras Stones are also accumulated passively over time via event rewards, and regular grinding (PVE).

---

## 5. Alternative Nodes vs. Sheep Mountain

If specific materials like **Cotton**, **Aloe**, **Herbs**, **Ores** or **Gems** are needed for Crafting or Processing rather than pure Caphras farming, alternative locations are available:

* **Kasula Farm (East of Tarif):** Cotton Gathering.
* **Ahto Farm:** Cotton and Aloe Gathering.
* **Lumbering (Calpheon / Treant Forest):** High Caphras yield, but incurs **extreme weight limits**, requiring continuous storage maid usage or high weight investment.
* **Hoe Gathering (Behr):** High Caphras yield as well as Everlasting Herb for Mythical Horse Awakening and other Alchemy recipes.
* **Mining (Pilgrim's Haven):** A popular gathering spot for all kinds of Ores and Gems used in Processing and Crafting certain items as well as grinding Life Skill EXP.
---

## 6. Standard Gathering Loop

1. Arrive at **Lynch Ranch Sheep Mountain**.
2. Activate **Agris Fever** and **Level 2 Item Scroll**.
3. Group sheep using **Gather 'Round!** (or equivalent class pull skill).
4. Slay the group and use the **Fluid Collector** while holding the interaction key (`R`).
5. Complete **Mini-Game prompts** instantly whenever they pop up for 10x drops.
6. Deposit accumulated fluids into a campsite anvil/storage or maid interface when necessary.
