# WEALTH (SILVER) ACHIEVEMENTS AND REWARDS

Melissia Games offers exclusive title rewards based on your total amount of raw/liquid silver.

## SILVER THRESHOLDS AND TITLE REWARDS

- **10 Trillion Silver**: `Unrivaled`

- **50 Trillion Silver**: `Godlike`

- **100 Trillion Silver**: `Gosu`

- **250 Trillion Silver**: `Light`

- **500 Trillion Silver**: `Darkness`

## REQUIREMENTS & INFORMATION

- Amounts must be held in liquid/raw silver. Items, bullion, or other trade assets of equivalent value do not qualify.

- These rewards are granted upon reaching each milestone; your silver will remain in your inventory.

- Once you hit any of the thresholds, notify the staff team to claim your title.

- Multi-tier claims are supported. For example, reaching 500 Trillion Silver entitles you to retroactively claim all preceding titles (10T, 50T, 100T, and 250T) at once, or you may claim them progressively over time.

- There are no time limits, claim caps, or leaderboard constraints. You may claim your rewards whenever you choose.
