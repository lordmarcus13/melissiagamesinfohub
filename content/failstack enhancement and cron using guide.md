## CORE ENHANCEMENT LOGIC
Enhancing in Melissia Games is built on two massive advantages: a **x10 Base Success Rate** and an accelerated **Anvil (Pity) System**. To enhance efficiently, you must adapt your strategy based on the gear tier:
1. **Early Game (Naru/Tuvala/Boss):** Brute-force your upgrades. The x10 base chance carries you. Save your Failstacks (FS) and Cron Stones.
2. **Mid Game (Blackstar/Deboreka):** Balance is key. Use moderate Failstacks and start using Crons to protect high-value items from degrading or shattering.
3. **Endgame (Sovereign/Slumbering Origin/Kharazad):** The **Anvil System** becomes your primary progression path. High Failstacks only offer a minimal success boost. **Expect to fail.** Your actual objective is to use Cron Stones to prevent gear downgrades while safely maxing out your Anvil meter for a 100% guaranteed upgrade.

Do **not** waste Failstacks or Cron Stones on these. Thanks to the x10 base success rate, you can easily brute-force both gear types all the way to **PEN (V)** just by tapping repeatedly.

## ⚠️ CRITICAL MECHANIC: THE ANVIL SYSTEM
**For all gear listed below, filling the Anvil meter is the definitive foundation of the enhancement system.** 
Despite hitting the recommended Failstacks, your enhancement attempts **will** frequently fail. At these tiers, Failstacks only provide a minimal bump to your success rate. While these numbers represent the optimal setup, Melissia Games’ x10 Base Chance and Anvil Boost are designed to make the pity system your primary win condition. 

**Your strategy:** Spam attempts, always use Crons to prevent drops, and let the Anvil fill up. 
> **[Must Read: Deep-dive into the Anvil System and Tables here](https://melissiagamesinfohub.vercel.app/en/wiki/enhancement)**

## KHARAZAD ACCESSORIES
You do not enhance these from scratch. You obtain them directly at **NOV (IX)** via an exchange quest. Hand over a **PEN (V) Deboreka Accessory** to the NPC **Clorince** in Velia (located to the right of the stable).

**Pre-requisites for the exchange quest:**
1. Open the Quest window (`O`) and ensure **"All"** quest types are toggled on at the bottom.
2. The PEN Deboreka must be in your inventory (unequipped).
3. The PEN Deboreka must be completely clean (No cups/reform stones applied).
