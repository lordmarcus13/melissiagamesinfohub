# Leveling: Savage Rift (Unique-Easiest)

Savage Rift provides a structured, highly efficient, instanced method for rapid leveling. It requires significantly less preparation than open-world grinding and is the absolute easiest path to high levels, making it a staple for pushing characters to Lv. 70.

### Important Note on EXP Scaling
Savage Rift yields exceptional Combat EXP, but it is vital to understand its progression mechanics: **the experience gained does not scale indefinitely with stage progression.** After successfully clearing Stage 3, the EXP reward hits its ceiling, meaning the amount of experience earned per stage remains completely static for the remainder of the rift.

### Expected Progression Rates
* **Lv. 59 to Lv. 65:** You will average approximately 2 Level Ups per run. A fresh Lv. 59 character can reach Lv. 65 in just 3 complete runs.
* **Lv. 66+:** You will earn approximately 2% to 4% Combat XP per run. 
* **Time Investment:** A single run typically takes between 3 to 15 minutes depending on team efficiency.

### How to Enter Savage Rift
Because this is an instanced matchmaking activity, you will need a minimum of 3 players (yourself + 2 others) to initiate a run.

1. Open your main menu by pressing `ESC`.
2. Navigate to the **War** tab, then select **Savage Rift** (Alternatively, you can use the menu search bar and type `Savage Rifts`).
3. Click the `Enter` button to queue into the instance.
