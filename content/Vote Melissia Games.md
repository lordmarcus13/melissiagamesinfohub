## [Click to Vote Melissia Games](https://office.melissia.games/account/vote)

**Voting for Melissia Games on gtop100 rewards 100 Cash (Acoin), and voting on ArenaTop100 rewards 50 Cash (Acoin). Each platform allows one vote per day**

1. Log in to your account at [Melissia Games Office](https://office.melissia.games/account/vote)


2. Click right top drop down menu `Account` and click on `Vote`

![](https://i.ibb.co/yBBKtCnm/image.png)

3. You will see 2 platforms to vote:
    - gtop100 - 100 Cash (Acoin) per day
    - ArenaTop100 - 50 Cash (Acoin) per day

![](https://i.ibb.co/tMCYR4GB/image.png)

4. Vote on gtop100

![](https://i.ibb.co/XxvjMZ3v/image.png)

5. **After you voted use `Back` button from browser to return to vote page and vote on ArenaTop100**
> You basically need to click `Back` twice from you browser. Sometimes office.melissia.games may not be loaded if you open it as new tab. Because you already have an active season.

6. Then complete voting by clicking `Vote` button on ArenaTop100
