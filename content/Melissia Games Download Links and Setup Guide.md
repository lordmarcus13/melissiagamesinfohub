## If Google Drive encounters a Download Limit Issue

### Click on `File` at the very top.

![](https://i.imgur.com/C74NbTZ.png)


### Select `Add to starred`.

![](https://i.imgur.com/1Pp6j2f.png)


### Open your Drive and create a `New Folder`.

![](https://i.imgur.com/IybP7hR.png)
> Name it whatever you want (e.g., Melissia, Melissia folder, etc.)


### Open the ➡ **Starred** tab in Drive.

![](https://i.imgur.com/zqgkFUc.png)

### Click on the 3 dots to the right of the Client file you starred (Melissia Test Client.7z).

### Go to `Organize` ➡ `Add shortcut`.

![](https://i.imgur.com/J6vfHZU.png)


### Select the newly created folder as the destination for the shortcut.

![](https://i.imgur.com/KSGtRZj.png)


### Open your Drive again. Right-click on the **folder you created that contains the shortcut** (*without opening the folder, directly on the folder itself*) and select `Download`.

![](https://i.imgur.com/pQ63b9f.png)
> You need to download the folder itself. Google Drive will automatically zip the folder. This is a well-known method to bypass Google's download limit/quota.
